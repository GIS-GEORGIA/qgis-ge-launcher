## English

This extension **does not collect, store or transmit** any personal data.

- **Network requests.** The extension never contacts any server. Every file
  (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, icons) ships inside the extension. It does not even
  fetch site favicons — it draws coloured letter tiles instead.
- **What is stored.** Only your own list of links and your UI preferences
  (language, theme, view). These live in `chrome.storage` inside your browser.
  If you have browser sync enabled, the list syncs across your own profile
  through the browser vendor's mechanism (Google / Microsoft); we never see it.
- **Permissions.** `storage` only. No access to browsing history, tab contents,
  or the sites you visit. Opening a link in a new tab needs no extra permission.
- **Analytics, ads, cookies.** None.
- **Third parties.** Nothing is shared, because nothing is collected.

Deletion: removing the extension from your browser deletes all stored data.
